Built on six foundations.

Today, all six

  • Works with no network

    Phones talk directly, phone to phone (D2D). Reaches significantly farther than standard device-to-device (D2D) links.

  • Voice and data together

    Voice, SOS, position, messages, photos and short clips on one link.

  • Voice in about a second

    From speaking to hearing, measured on our test phones, October 2026.

  • Forms on the spot

    Phones form the group themselves.

  • Privacy built in

    Nothing goes through a server. A warning to people nearby carries no identity.

  • Sees what eyes cannot

    Works behind a bus, a wall or a corner.

Today

Delivered as an SDK inside an existing app.

Vision

It will be part of the phone itself.
