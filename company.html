Protecting the unpredictable.

Vizible Zone was founded in 2019 by veterans of mission-critical group communications for emergency and rescue services. We make the phones people already carry aware of danger, and able to tell whoever it concerns.

What we believe

Machines can be engineered and connected. People cannot.

In June 2019 we put it this way: “while cars will be connected and controlled, human behavior near roads will still be unpredictable.” By the end of that year it was three words: protecting the unpredictable. The same five ideas run through everything we have built since.

  • Make the unseen person visible.
  • Use the phone people already carry.
  • No cellular network in between.
  • Warn the other party.
  • People’s behaviour is the unpredictable part.

Where it began

One child, one car, one moment. The idea has not changed; the worlds it serves have grown.

The child behind the bus

Two months later, in February 2019, our application to an incubator gave the same phones a second purpose: keeping public-safety teams in touch “when the cellular network collapses in a disaster”. Seven years later, that is our public-safety product: push-to-talk and SOS when cellular networks fail.

Milestones

  1. 2019

    Founded. First field demonstrations: a phone warns a car about a person it cannot see.

  2. 2019–20

    In-car demonstrations at night and behind obstacles, and the Helsinki Jätkäsaari Mobility Lab pilot.

  3. 2020

    Shown at CES 2020.

  4. 2020

    Contact tracing for workplaces, to a spec defined by the Israeli Ministries of Defense & Healthcare. A partnership with Verint.

  5. 2021

    Exposure notification for Mekorot, Israel’s national water company.

  6. 2023–24

    A demonstration for Mitsubishi Corporation and Yazaki.

  7. 2026

    Push-to-talk, SOS and awareness with no network. Shown at the Smart Mobility Summit in Tel Aviv.
