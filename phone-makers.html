Three everyday moments

In preparation.

The situation

A family spread out on a ski slope, and no network.

Nobody can call anybody. Elsewhere, someone walks towards the road with music on and eyes on the screen. In both moments the phone could help, and says nothing.

12Awareness and understanding

Their phones keep talking, because they never needed the network.

Today: push-to-talk with family and friends, phone to phone, where there is no network.

Today: the phone knows what its person is doing.

In development: with music on, eyes on the screen, one step from the road, it will say “Look up”.

3Prediction

It will see him about to step into the road, before his foot leaves the kerb.

In development: the phone will see that step coming.

4Warning

It will tell him to look up.

In development: his phone will say “Look up”.

The phone will never tell him it is safe to cross: it will warn, and he will decide.

Who is told: the distracted person. Nobody else.

What you get

A feature your users feel the day the network is gone, and a phone that will look out for them.

Today: push-to-talk with family and friends where there is no network, on a ski slope, a trail or a festival field.

In development: the phone will look out for its person at a crossing.

Vision: it will do the same at the top of an escalator, and notice dangers that start inside the phone itself, telling its owner how sure it is.
