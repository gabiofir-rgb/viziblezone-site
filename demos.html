Each capability on this site, grouped by world, with a short illustration of what it does. Illustrations, not recordings.

Public safety

  • Group push-to-talk One person talks, and the whole group hears, in about a second (measured on our test phones, October 2026). Status: Today
  • Utilities and field crews Push-to-talk and messages where there is no network. Status: Today
  • SOS with position One press sends a call for help, with your available position, to teammates on supported phones in coverage. In our tests it arrived within seconds, even indoors (measured on our test phones, November 2026). Status: Today
  • Team map The available positions of teammates in coverage, and how far away they are. Status: Today
  • Messages, photos and short clips Over the same direct link. Status: Today
  • Private groups, joined by a touch Two phones touch, and a teammate is in. A phone from another group does not hear yours. Status: Today
  • Ready while asleep With the screen off, the phone still wakes to the group’s voice. Status: Today
  • Inside the apps people already have Delivered as an SDK inside an existing app, such as an emergency app, so nobody installs anything on the day of a disaster. Status: Today
  • Automatic trapped mode A trapped person’s phone opens a voice and data channel. Rescuers can hear and answer them. Status: Today
  • An alert across the city One alert from the command post will reach phones across a whole city, with no network. Shown so far in computer simulation. Status: Vision
  • SOS beyond the team A call for help will carry its position to the people nearby and to the nearest command post, anywhere in the city. Status: Vision
  • A message to one person A message will travel across the city to one phone, one group or the command post, and the sender will know it arrived. Status: Vision
  • Shelters and places indoors Alerts will also reach people sheltering indoors. Status: Vision
  • Built into the phone It will be part of the phone itself, for every user, with no app to install. Status: Vision

The labels

  • Today

    Works now, and we can show it to you.

  • In development

    Being built, or shown so far in the lab or in field trials. Not a product yet.

  • Vision

    Where we are going. Not built yet.

How we label what we claim

Closed compounds

  • Drivers warned about workers on foot Even out of sight, with an aircraft between them. Status: Today
  • Around a parked aircraft The aircraft carries small tags and gets its own bubble. Status: Today
  • Around a danger area A driver nearing a tagged danger area, such as an open pit, is warned. Status: Today
  • At the runway hold line At an airport, the hold line can be tagged as a danger area. A driver nearing it is warned. Status: Today
  • Rules per area and per role The site will decide who may enter each area, and who will be warned. Status: In development
  • Seeing the meeting coming It will warn before the paths meet, not only on entry. Status: In development
  • Seaports, rail, logistics and construction sites The same capability will be set up with each site, in a pilot first. Status: In development
  • Workers on foot warned too Workers on foot will be warned too. Status: Vision
  • Bubbles that switch on or move Bubbles will appear around an engine at start-up, a fuelling stand or an aircraft under tow. Status: Vision
  • A private record for each driver It will be kept on the driver’s own phone. Status: Vision

Smart cities

  • Inside the city’s own app Delivered as an SDK inside the city app residents already use, so the cars around them know they are there. Status: Today
  • The person outside warned too The person on foot, on a bike or on a scooter will be warned on the phone. Status: Vision
  • Adaptive crossing Traffic lights will give a slow walker more time. Status: Vision
  • A city view of risky spots The city will see where people cross away from a crossing and where close calls happen, by place and time, with no names. Status: Vision
  • Smart lighting The same awareness will serve smart lighting. Status: Vision

On the road

  • Sees what eyes cannot Behind a bus, a wall or a corner, and in the dark. It does not rely on seeing; rain and fog are still to be measured. Status: Today
  • Warnings about people the driver cannot see The driver is told about a person on foot, on a bike or a scooter, with the side they come from and how urgent it is. Shown in field demonstrations with cars since 2019. Status: Today
  • On the car’s own screen The warning appears on the car’s own screen, as in our in-car demonstrations, among them one for Mitsubishi Corporation and Yazaki in 2023–⁠24. Status: Today
  • Earlier, with prediction With prediction, more time to warn before someone crosses. Status: Vision
  • Into fleets and driver assistance The same warning will reach fleet systems and driver-assistance features. Status: Vision

Phone makers

  • Push-to-talk with family and friends Family and friends talk phone to phone where there is no network. Status: Today
  • Crossing with music on It will notice its person heading into the road and say “Look up”. Status: In development
  • The top of the steps It will say “Mind the step” before its person misses one. Status: Vision
  • A fall It will notice a fall and call for help. Status: Vision
  • A phone that knows itself It will notice its own abnormal behaviour and say how sure it is. Status: Vision
  • Inside the phone itself It will be part of the phone itself, for every user, with no app to install. Status: Vision

Contact tracing

  • Distancing alerts A warning when people stand too close for too long. Status: Today
  • Exposure notice The people who were really close are told. Status: Today
  • Rules the organisation sets How close, how long, how many days. Status: Today
  • A console for the organisation Activity and reports. Status: Today
  • Encounter records on each phone Records stay on each person’s phone. When a case is confirmed, that person’s record is shared with the organisation. Status: Today
