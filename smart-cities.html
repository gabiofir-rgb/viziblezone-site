The situation

A car turns right across the bike lane. A woman on a scooter is coming up in its blind spot.

Every street has spots like this: a bus at the stop, a van at the kerb, a corner, a stretch with no light. With Vizible Zone, the car knows she is there.

12Awareness and understanding

Her phone and the car know about each other, even out of sight.

It does not rely on seeing, so a bus, a corner or the dark do not hide anyone.

Today: it knows whether its person is walking, crossing, or riding a bike or a scooter.

4Warning

It tells the turning driver.

Today: the driver is warned on a screen in the car: “Scooter on the right”.

Vision: the person outside will be warned on the phone.

Nothing carries her name, and the message is gone once they have passed.

Who is told: the turning driver. Nobody else.

What you get

The driver is warned about people the car cannot see yet.

Today: the driver gets the warning on a screen in the car, about children, seniors, cyclists and scooter riders.

Vision: the person outside will be warned on the phone; you will see where your streets put people at risk, by place and time, with no names; and traffic lights will give a slow walker more time.
